The Space

Light-filled, warm, and unlike anywhere else in Portland.

Housed in the historic 1946 Atomic Garage, Willow Haus features soaring arched ceilings and a wall of south-facing windows that flood the space with natural light throughout the day. Inside you'll find a full kitchen with a large island, a styled living room with a working electric fireplace, and flexible table-and-chair configurations for workshops, meetings, and gatherings of all kinds.

It's the kind of space that makes your team actually want to show up — and that looks stunning in photos.
