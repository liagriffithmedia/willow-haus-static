The Space

Light-filled, warm, and unlike anywhere else in Portland.

Housed in the historic 1949 Atomic Garage, Willow Haus features soaring arched ceilings and a wall of south-facing windows that flood the space with natural light throughout the day. This isn't a blank canvas—it's a thoughtfully designed and curated space, complete with a custom kitchen and large island, curated art, styled furnishings, seating for up to 36 at the main table, plus a curated living room and library nook perfect for smaller conversations and intimate gatherings.

It's the kind of space that makes your guests actually want to show up—and that looks stunning in photos.
